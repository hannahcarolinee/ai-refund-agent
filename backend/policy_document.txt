================================================================================
                    APEX COMMERCE GLOBAL REFUND POLICY (POL-2025-REV4)
================================================================================

1. TIME WINDOW ELIGIBILITY
   1.1. Refund requests must be initiated within 30 calendar days from the verified
        carrier delivery date.
   1.2. Items delivered more than 30 calendar days ago are strictly ineligible 
        for refund, return, or store credit.

2. PRODUCT CONDITION & RESTOCKING DEDUCTIONS
   2.1. Unopened / Factory-Sealed: 100% full refund to original payment method.
   2.2. Opened / Like-New: Eligible for a refund with a mandatory 15% restocking fee
        deducted, OR 100% store credit (customer choice).
   2.3. Damaged / Heavily Used: Ineligible for refund unless covered under DOA (Section 5.2).

3. NON-REFUNDABLE CATEGORIES (EXCLUSIONS)
   3.1. Digital products, downloadable software licenses, and digital gift cards.
   3.2. Personal hygiene, swimwear, and intimate apparel if packaging is unsealed.
   3.3. Clearance or "Final Sale" items discounted at 50% or greater.
   3.4. Custom-made, monogrammed, or personalized merchandise.

4. FRAUD & ABUSE GUARDRAILS
   4.1. Account Risk Threshold: Accounts with fraud_risk_score >= 0.70 must be
        escalated to a human supervisor. The agent cannot issue automated refunds.
   4.2. Frequency Lock: Accounts with 3 or more refunds in the past 90 days are 
        locked from automated processing and require supervisor review.
   4.3. High-Value Cap: Single items exceeding $500.00 require human escalation.

5. SPECIAL CIRCUMSTANCES
   5.1. Lost in Transit: Tracking shows no movement for >= 7 business days:
        100% full refund or free replacement.
   5.2. Damaged on Arrival (DOA): Full refund if reported within 72 hours of delivery.
================================================================================
